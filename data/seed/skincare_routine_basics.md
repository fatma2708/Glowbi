# Skincare routine basics
A simple routine beats a complicated one. The core is three steps: cleanse, moisturize, and protect with sunscreen in the morning.
Morning: gentle cleanser (or just water if your skin is dry), optional antioxidant serum such as vitamin C, moisturizer, then broad-spectrum sunscreen SPF 30 or higher.
Evening: cleanse (double cleanse with an oil or balm first if you wore sunscreen or makeup), treatment step (retinoid, exfoliating acid or a targeted serum), then moisturizer.
Order products from thinnest to thickest texture. Sunscreen is the last step of the morning routine, before makeup.
Introduce only one new product at a time and wait about two weeks before adding another, so you know what works and what irritates.
Patch test new products on a small area, such as behind the ear or the inner arm, for a few days before using them on the whole face.
