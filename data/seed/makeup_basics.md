# Makeup basics
Good makeup starts with prepped skin: moisturize and let sunscreen settle a few minutes before foundation to avoid pilling.
To find a foundation shade, swatch on the jawline in daylight and check your undertone: cool (pink), warm (yellow or golden) or neutral.
Oily skin often prefers matte, long-wear foundations and a light powder on the T-zone. Dry skin looks best with dewy or satin finishes and hydrating primers.
Clean makeup brushes and sponges every week or two; dirty tools can contribute to breakouts.
Replace mascara about every three months and liquid eye products when they change smell or texture, to reduce the risk of eye infections.
Always remove makeup before sleeping, ideally with a cleansing balm or micellar water followed by a gentle cleanser.
