# Nail care basics
Keep nails moisturized with cuticle oil or hand cream, especially after washing hands.
Don't cut cuticles aggressively; gently push them back after a shower instead, because the cuticle protects against infection.
File in one direction with a fine file to prevent splitting. Give nails short breaks from gel manicures if they become thin or peel, and never peel gel polish off.
Wear gloves when cleaning or washing dishes to protect nails from water and detergents.
