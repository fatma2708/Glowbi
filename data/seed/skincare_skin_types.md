# Skin types and what they need
Oily skin produces more sebum and looks shiny, often with visible pores. Lightweight gel or fluid moisturizers, non-comedogenic products and ingredients like niacinamide and salicylic acid (BHA) help. Skipping moisturizer usually makes things worse, not better.
Dry skin feels tight and can flake. It benefits from cream cleansers, humectants such as glycerin and hyaluronic acid, plus occlusives and emollients like ceramides, squalane and shea butter to lock moisture in.
Combination skin is oily in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) and normal or dry on the cheeks. Use a balanced moisturizer and apply richer products only where needed.
Sensitive skin reacts easily with redness, stinging or itching. Choose fragrance-free formulas with short ingredient lists, avoid harsh scrubs, and introduce actives slowly.
Dehydrated skin lacks water rather than oil, and any skin type can be dehydrated. Signs are tightness and dullness even when the skin is oily.
