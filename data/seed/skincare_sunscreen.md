# Sunscreen essentials
Sunscreen is the most effective anti-aging and anti-dark-spot step. Use broad spectrum (UVA and UVB) protection, SPF 30 or more, every day, even when it is cloudy.
Use about two finger lengths for the face and neck. Reapply every two hours when outdoors, and after swimming or sweating.
Mineral filters (zinc oxide, titanium dioxide) suit sensitive skin but may leave a white cast on deeper skin tones. Chemical filters are usually more cosmetically elegant. Tinted sunscreens with iron oxides also help against visible light, which matters for melasma.
Makeup with SPF is not enough on its own because people apply far less than the tested amount.
