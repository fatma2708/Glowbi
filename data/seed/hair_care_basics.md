# Hair care basics
Shampoo is for the scalp and conditioner is for the lengths and ends. Focus shampoo at the roots and conditioner from mid-length down.
Curly and coily hair is naturally drier because scalp oils travel less easily down the strand. Wash less often, use sulfate-free or gentle cleansers, deep condition regularly and consider methods like leave-in plus a cream or gel to define curls.
Fine or oily hair benefits from lightweight conditioners and washing as often as needed; heavy oils can weigh it down.
Heat styling damages the cuticle: use a heat protectant, keep tools under about 185 degrees Celsius when possible, and avoid flat ironing wet hair.
Frizz is usually a sign of dryness or humidity. Smoothing products with silicones or oils, and drying with a microfiber towel or cotton t-shirt, help.
Dandruff is often managed with shampoos containing zinc pyrithione, ketoconazole, selenium sulfide or salicylic acid. Sudden or patchy hair loss should be checked by a doctor or dermatologist.
